ClipForge Terms of Service

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ClipForge is a personal desktop application. It edits video clips into vertical short-form videos and, when the user chooses to, sends them to the user's own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account.

Use

ClipForge is used only by its operator on their own computer and their own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Content

The user is responsible for having the right to use any video they edit or upload with ClipForge, including permission from creators through clipping programs. Uploaded content must follow the terms and community guidelines of each platform, including the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

TikTok integration

ClipForge uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the user's TikTok account and the Content Posting API to send videos to the user's TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published without the user finishing the post in the TikTok app.

YouTube and Instagram

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services to upload videos the user selects to the user's own channel. By using ClipForge you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Instagram publishing follows Meta's Terms and Instagram's Community Guidelines.
